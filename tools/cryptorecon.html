Applied Crypto

cryptorecon

In development·Python·github/sparwol
Design phase.

A cryptographic posture scanner for the reconnaissance phase. Enumerates TLS versions, cipher suites, certificate practices, and crypto-agility indicators across a target's external surface, then maps the findings to attack-surface implications rather than just listing them.

Input
Target domain or IP range
Sources
Active TLS probing + Certificate Transparency logs
Output
Posture report with attack-surface mapping
Language
Python 3.x

What It Does

Beyond a standard TLS scanner, cryptorecon is built to answer the operator's question — not "what cipher suites are enabled" but "where does the crypto debt point me." It correlates weak TLS endpoints with the services behind them, mines Certificate Transparency logs for forgotten subdomains and leaked internal hostnames, and flags the absence of crypto-agility as a long-game indicator.
